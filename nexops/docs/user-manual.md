# NexOps User Manual

This guide covers everything a regular user needs to know to use NexOps.

## Creating an account

1. Go to the NexOps login page
2. Click the link to switch to the register form
3. Fill in your name, email, password, department, and rank
4. Leave the role set to User, unless an admin told you otherwise
5. Click Register, then log in with your new account

## Logging in

1. Enter your email and password on the login page
2. Click Log In
3. You will land on your dashboard, showing your own tickets only

## Submitting a ticket

1. From your dashboard, click Tickets
2. Fill in a short title describing the issue
3. Write a clear description, the more detail you give, the faster it gets resolved
4. Click Get AI Suggestion to have the system suggest a category and priority,
   or set them yourself if you prefer
5. Click Submit Ticket

## Checking your ticket status

Your dashboard always shows your most recent tickets, along with their current status,
open, in progress, resolved, or closed. Click on a ticket to see its full details.

## Updating your settings

1. Go to Settings from the navigation menu
2. Update your name, email, or department as needed, then click Save Profile
3. To change your profile picture, click the file selector next to your current picture
   and choose a new image, it uploads automatically
4. To change your password, scroll to the Change Password section, enter your current
   password and a new one, then click Change Password

## Forgot your password

1. On the login page, click Forgot Password
2. Enter the email associated with your account
3. Check your email for a reset link, it expires after 1 hour
4. Click the link and set a new password

## Getting help

If you run into an issue using NexOps itself, contact your system administrator directly,
since account and access issues need to be handled by someone with admin permissions.
